I am a PhD student in Automotive Engineering for Intelligent Mobility at the University of Bologna, supervised by Prof. Stefano Mattoccia at the Computer Vision Lab. My research is about vision-based perception for autonomous systems: end-to-end driving, depth estimation with event cameras, and metric visual odometry in natural environments, where GNSS is unreliable.

In 2026 I spent six months as a visiting researcher at Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg. Since 2020 I have been part of the UniBo Motorsport Driverless 1:10 team, which I have led since 2024. We won the RoboRacer World Championship at ICRA 2025.

Before the PhD I earned a BSc in Physics and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Bologna, spending the second year of the master's at NTNU in Trondheim.

Autonomous Racing

I joined the UniBo Motorsport Driverless 1:10 team in 2020 and have led it since 2024, coordinating about twenty bachelor's and master's students. We build and program 1:10 scale autonomous cars for the RoboRacer competitions held at ICRA: open practice to map the track, time trials, and a head-to-head tournament where detecting and overtaking the opponent is essential.

  • 1st place, RoboRacer World Championship, ICRA 2025, Atlanta
  • 2nd and 3rd place, Master Cup, RoboRacer World Championship, ICRA 2026, Vienna (29 teams)

For the 2026 season I replaced our pure-pursuit and Frenet overtaking stack with a single nonlinear Model Predictive Controller (CasADi + IPOPT): it follows the raceline and avoids walls and opponents in one optimisation, so overtaking emerges from the same cost function.
